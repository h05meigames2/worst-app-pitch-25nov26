Entry for the make.fun bounty **The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched**: one sentence
(≤240 characters) pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable — a product
that sounds real, a founder who sounds certain, and a function nobody needs.

The pitch: *Doorknob* — an app that lets busy professionals rate, review, and
follow their favorite doorknobs, so you'll never turn a handle again without
knowing its grip, spin, and five-star potential.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the entry to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (defaults to `/output`).
